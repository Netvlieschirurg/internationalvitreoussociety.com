Add it to Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on a computer, signed in with your IVS address.
  2. Click the gear icon at the top right, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it IVS.
  4. Click into the signature box and paste: Cmd+V on a Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows.
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose IVS for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  6. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save Changes.

Above the quoted text

Tick Insert signature before quoted text in replies, so the signature sits directly under your reply.

Sending as info@

If you can send from info@, pick that address in the menu under Signature defaults and choose the society signature for it.

Gmail on your phone

The Gmail app has its own text-only signature, set in the app under Settings for your IVS account.
